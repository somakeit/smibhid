PMSA003I Particulate Matter Sensor Management

Monitor and configure your PMSA003I laser particle sensor. The sensor measures PM1.0, PM2.5, and PM10 concentrations plus particle counts across six size bins. Fan duty cycling reduces wear while maintaining accurate readings at configurable intervals.

🎯 Range: 0–500 μg/m³ (PM2.5), 0–1000 μg/m³ (PM10)
📊 Particle sizes: 0.3, 0.5, 1.0, 2.5, 5.0, 10 μm bins

Configure how often the sensor takes readings and how long the fan runs per cycle. Longer sleep periods reduce fan wear.

Configuration Notes:

  • Fan run time ≥ 10s recommended (datasheet response time)
  • Changes take effect on the next duty cycle
  • Settings are not persisted across restarts
